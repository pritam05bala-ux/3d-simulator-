Smart Library Seat Finder: 3D circuit

ESP32 DevKit, four FSR pressure sensors, each in a voltage divider with a 10 kΩ resistor. Click a sensor to press it.

Wires
3V3 to FSR leg 1
FSR leg 2 + resistor to GPIO
Resistor to GND

Live readings

Drag to rotate, scroll or pinch to zoom, click a round sensor to press it. Voltages are approximate. Pins 32 to 35 are ADC1, which keeps working while WiFi is on.
